About

I am an Associate Professor at the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA). Before joining UTA, I was an Assistant Professor at the State University of New York (SUNY) at Binghamton. I received my Ph.D. degree from Purdue University, where I researched in the FRIENDS Lab under the direction of Professor Dongyan Xu.

I work on the systems behind cloud computing and AI and am particularly drawn to practical problems: How can we keep workloads isolated without slowing them down, move data without disrupting applications, and make better use of the hardware we have? These questions guide my work on memory, storage, networking, virtualization, and large-scale AI training and inference. I am the recipient of an NSF CAREER Award (2023)
