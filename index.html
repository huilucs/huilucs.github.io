I am an Associate Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Texas at Arlington. Before joining UTA, I was an Assistant Professor at the State University of New York at Binghamton. I received my Ph.D. from Purdue University, where I worked in the FRIENDS Lab under the direction of Professor Dongyan Xu.

My research focuses on making cloud and AI infrastructure faster, safer, and more efficient. My interests span operating systems, systems virtualization, memory and storage systems, and computer networks. Recent work explores lightweight secure containers, transparent memory tiering across DRAM, CXL, and accelerators, high-throughput networking, and systems support for large-scale model training and inference. I am the recipient of an NSF CAREER Award (2023). Much of my work is motivated by real deployment needs and carried out in collaboration with industry partners.

Prospective students: Motivated students interested in systems research are welcome to contact me at hui.lu@uta.edu.
